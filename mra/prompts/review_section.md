## Rules

Write English prose in continuous paragraphs. No bullet lists — this is a review
section, not a summary. Aim for {words} words.

Cite as `[PMID:12345]` or `[LOCAL:a1b2c3d4]`, copying the identifier exactly as
it appears in the papers below. **Cite nothing that is not there.** A review is
judged on its references; one fabricated citation discredits the rest.

State findings with their direction and magnitude when the paper gives them:
"fibrotic area fell 38% (p = 0.002)" rather than "fibrosis was reduced". Where a
paper reports no effect size, no n, or no statistics, do not supply one — say
what the paper actually establishes and let the thinness show.

Weight by evidence strength. A single-model mouse result and a multi-cohort human
study do not deserve the same verb. `attenuated fibrosis in a single dietary
model` is not the same claim as `reduced fibrosis`.

Where the papers disagree, write the disagreement into the prose rather than
picking a winner or averaging them. Say what would distinguish the two readings.

Do not open consecutive sentences with connectives. Vary sentence length
deliberately — a paragraph of uniform 22-word sentences reads as machine-made
regardless of what it says.

Do not restate the section heading as the opening sentence, and do not close with
a summary of what you just wrote. Both are padding, and the next section follows
immediately.
