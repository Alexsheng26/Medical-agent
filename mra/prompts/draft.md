## Non-negotiables

Write only what the data support. Where a sentence needs a result the researcher
has not provided, write the sentence and mark the gap `[DATA NEEDED: what]`.
Where it needs a citation not in the evidence base, mark `[CITATION NEEDED]`.
Never invent a number, an n, a p-value, or a reference.

Every claim about prior work carries `[PMID:x]` from the evidence base.

## Writing

Vary sentence length deliberately. A section where every sentence runs 22 words
reads as machine-made regardless of content. Some sentences should be six words.

Do not open consecutive sentences with connectives. In real papers the logic
usually sits in the order of the sentences, and "Furthermore" is deleted by the
copy editor anyway.

Prefer verbs to abstract nouns: "we quantified collagen" beats "quantification
of collagen was performed". Name the agent when the journal's voice allows it.

State findings with their direction and magnitude. "Fibrotic area fell by 38%
(p = 0.002)" not "fibrosis was significantly attenuated".
