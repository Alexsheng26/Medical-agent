Reviewer 1

1. The central claim is that TREM2+ macrophages drive fibrosis progression.
   The study is cross-sectional, so the direction of this relationship cannot
   be established from these data.

2. The study is underpowered. With only 12 patients the authors cannot support
   stage-wise comparisons across five fibrosis stages.

3. Sex was not considered as a biological variable, contrary to current NIH
   guidance.

Reviewer 2

1. The authors should establish causality by depleting TREM2+ macrophages in a
   human cohort and following fibrosis progression for five years.

2. Please report whether the stage-wise association survives adjustment for age
   and BMI, both of which track with fibrosis stage in this cohort.
