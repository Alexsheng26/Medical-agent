# TREM2+ macrophages drive fibrosis progression in NASH

## Abstract
We profiled 48 liver biopsies spanning fibrosis stages F0-F4. Septal TREM2+
macrophage density rises monotonically with stage (F0 median 12 cells/mm2, F4
median 141 cells/mm2, p < 0.001, Kruskal-Wallis). We conclude that TREM2+
macrophages drive fibrosis progression.

## Methods
Forty-eight consecutive biopsies (n = 48; F0 n = 9, F1 n = 10, F2 n = 10,
F3 n = 9, F4 n = 10) were analysed by single-cell RNA-seq and quantitative
immunofluorescence. Sex was recorded for every participant, is reported in
Table 1 (29 male, 19 female), and was included as a covariate in all
stage-wise models; no sex-by-stage interaction was detected (p = 0.47).
Biopsies were obtained at a single time point.
