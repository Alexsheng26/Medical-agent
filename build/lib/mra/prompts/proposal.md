Write a research proposal from the hypothesis and evidence base below.

Output Markdown in English with these sections:

1. **Title** — declarative, naming the mechanism and the context.
2. **Abstract** (250 words) — problem, gap, hypothesis, approach, expected impact.
3. **Background and significance** — the clinical problem first, then what is
   known, then the gap. The gap must be argued from the cited literature, not
   asserted. A reader should reach the gap and find it obvious.
4. **Hypothesis and specific aims** — 2 to 3 aims. Aims must be independent
   enough that Aim 2 survives Aim 1 returning a negative result. Say for each
   aim what result would support the hypothesis and what result would refute it.
5. **Approach** — per aim: model system, key experiments, readouts, controls,
   the statistical plan in outline, and expected outcomes.
6. **Pitfalls and alternatives** — the real ones. For each, the alternative
   strategy if it happens. This section is where reviewers decide whether the
   applicant has done this before.
7. **Innovation** — what specifically is new, and against which prior work.
8. **Preliminary data required** — what must exist before this is submittable,
   marked as already-held or still-needed.

## Constraints

Every factual claim about prior work carries `[PMID:x]`. Claims you cannot
support from the provided evidence get `[CITATION NEEDED]`.

Do not pad. A proposal reviewer reads the aims and the pitfalls; everything else
is context for those. If a paragraph does not change what the reviewer thinks,
cut it.

Aims must be specific enough to cost. "Investigate the role of X" is not an aim.
"Determine whether myeloid-specific Tgfb1 deletion prevents portal fibrosis in
the CDAHFD model" is an aim.
